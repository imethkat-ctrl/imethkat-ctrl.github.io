Questions, answered plainly

Who owns the site?

You do. The files we deliver are yours. The domain is registered in your name. We don’t hold the site hostage.

What do you need from me?

Your truck name, cuisine or style, usual suburbs or events, phone, email, and a few photos of the truck and the food (phone photos are fine). Your menu, even as a list or a photo. If you have a website, Instagram, or a Google profile, send those links too, plus access to the Google profile if you want us to update it. That’s enough for a free demo.

What’s included in the A$399?

A booking page, your menu on that page, and a Google profile fix (hours, photos, description, and a review QR card). Live within 24 hours of payment, once you’ve approved the demo and given us what we need.

How do I pay?

After you approve the demo, we send a PayID invoice. Nothing is due before you say yes.

What if it isn’t live in 24 hours?

If you’ve approved the demo, paid, and given us what we need, and we don’t have the site live within 24 hours, you get a full refund.

Will this get me Christmas party bookings?

We put a clear “Book us for your Christmas party” hook on your page, with a simple way for people to enquire or book. That makes you easier to find and easier to hire. We don’t promise a set number of jobs, and we don’t use fake reviews or fake bookings to sell the work.

What happens after Christmas?

The page stays up. We can change the hook to general events, catering, or private bookings. If you take the optional care plan, menu and text updates go through that.
