About

Local, reliable, easy to reach

Ember Trestle is a fictional Melbourne food truck, used as a demo of a ready-made booking site. It is not a real business, and the phone number is a placeholder.

The site is built so a real truck could take Christmas-party and event bookings in 24 hours: a clear menu, a tap-to-call button, and a form that goes to the operator’s email. Before go-live we swap in real photos, a real menu, and a real number.
